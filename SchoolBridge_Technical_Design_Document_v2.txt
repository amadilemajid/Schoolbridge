1. Executive Summary
SchoolBridge is a WhatsApp-first school operations and revenue automation platform purpose-built for Ugandan schools. It addresses the most painful daily problems school administrators, bursars, teachers, and directors face: delayed fee collection, poor parent communication, manual academic reporting, and fragmented payment reconciliation.
Unlike generic school management software imported from abroad, SchoolBridge is designed from the ground up for the Ugandan school operating environment — integrating with MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money, supporting UNEB examination report formats, operating reliably on low-bandwidth connections, and communicating with parents through WhatsApp, the platform they already use daily.

Strategic Positioning
SchoolBridge is not a school management system. It is a school operations, communication, and revenue automation platform. Every feature is designed to either save administrator time, increase fee collection rates, or strengthen the school-parent relationship.
The platform targets Uganda's 20,000+ registered primary and secondary schools, the majority of which still manage fees manually, send paper report cards, and communicate with parents through unreliable individual phone calls.

2. Problem Statement
Uganda's school sector is large, growing, and chronically underserved by technology. The problems are systemic and well-documented:
2.1 Fee Collection Crisis
The average Ugandan school collects only 50–65% of expected fees per term. The root causes include:
• No automated reminder system — bursars rely on handwritten lists and verbal reminders
• No easy payment channel — parents must travel to school or send cash with children
• No real-time visibility — administrators cannot see who has paid at a glance
• No payment plans — parents with partial ability to pay are treated the same as non-payers

Financial Impact
A school with 500 students at UGX 400,000 per term expects UGX 200,000,000. At 60% collection, it actually receives UGX 120,000,000 — a UGX 80,000,000 shortfall every term. SchoolBridge targets a collection rate above 85%, recovering UGX 50,000,000+ per term for a school of this size.
2.2 Parent Communication Breakdown
• Schools have no reliable broadcast channel — announcements are sent via children or handwritten notes
• Parents have no visibility into academic performance between term report cards
• Fee balance inquiries require a physical visit to the school office
• Emergency alerts cannot reach all parents reliably
2.3 Academic Reporting Inefficiency
• Teachers enter marks in multiple registers and notebooks
• Report cards are handwritten or typed individually — a process taking 3–5 days per class
• No standardised digital record of academic performance over time
• UNEB-format reports (P7, UCE, UACE) require manual reformatting every term
2.4 Administrative Fragmentation
• Student enrolment data lives in physical registers
• Payment records are in separate ledgers from student records
• No single dashboard for school leadership to view operations
• Audit trails are non-existent — fraud and errors go undetected

3.2 Uganda-Specific Differentiation
This is SchoolBridge's most powerful and defensible advantage: deep local specificity that foreign SaaS products cannot replicate.
• UNEB format report cards — P7, UCE (S4), and UACE (S6) formats built in. Schools are legally required to issue these formats.
• MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money — the two dominant payment rails in Uganda, integrated natively
• WhatsApp-first communication — Uganda's most widely used messaging platform, requiring no app installation from parents
• SMS fallback — automated SMS when WhatsApp delivery fails, essential for low-connectivity areas
• Low-bandwidth mode — admin portal functions on 2G/3G connections with offline caching
• Uganda Shilling natively — no currency conversion, no foreign payment processor friction

4. Core System Architecture
SchoolBridge uses a hybrid cloud architecture optimised for reliability, low-latency, and graceful degradation in low-connectivity environments. The system is composed of five major layers:

4.1 Multi-Tenancy Model
SchoolBridge is a multi-tenant SaaS platform. Each school is an isolated tenant with its own data namespace, user roles, and configuration. Data isolation is enforced at the database row level using a school_id foreign key on all school-specific tables, with Django middleware validating tenant context on every request.
• Single shared schema with row-level isolation — cost-efficient and performant
• Each school's data is logically separated and never visible to other schools
• Super-admin accounts can access any tenant for support with a full audit trail
4.2 Offline and Low-Bandwidth Strategy
Critical Design Requirement
Uganda's internet connectivity is unreliable outside major urban centres. SchoolBridge must function usefully when the internet is slow or unavailable. This is not an optional enhancement — it is a core product requirement.
• Progressive Web App (PWA) with service worker for offline caching of the admin portal
• Critical read operations (student list, fee balances, attendance) available offline using IndexedDB
• Write operations queued locally and synced when connectivity resumes
• WhatsApp delivery failures automatically trigger SMS fallback within 2 minutes
• API responses compressed with gzip; images lazy-loaded and compressed

5.1 Admin Web Portal
The web portal is the primary operational interface for school staff, accessible via any modern browser on desktop, tablet, or smartphone. User roles determine which modules are visible and what actions are permitted.

Student Management Module
• Student profiles: name, date of birth, gender, class, stream, photo, parent contacts
• Enrolment, transfer, graduation, and withdrawal workflows
• Bulk import via CSV from existing manual registers
• Class and stream management with year-end promotion automation
• Search and filter by name, class, fee status, and academic performance

• Daily class attendance recorded by teacher on mobile or desktop
• Automated WhatsApp/SMS alert to parent when student is absent without excuse
• Weekly attendance summary reports per class
• Attendance rate tracking with alerts when a student falls below configurable threshold

5.2 Fee Management & Collection Module
This is the highest-value module and the primary driver of adoption. It automates the entire fee lifecycle from invoicing to reconciliation.
Fee Structure Configuration
• Configurable fee items per class level: tuition, lunch, transport, development, examination fees
• Term-based fee schedules (Term 1, 2, 3) with due dates
• Sibling discounts, scholarship deductions, and bursary flags
• Payment plans: define instalment schedules for families with confirmed partial payment arrangements
Payment Recording
• Manual cash payment recording by bursar with auto-generated receipt
• Automatic recording of Mobile Money payments via API webhook — no manual entry
• Each payment linked to a student account with timestamp, amount, channel, and operator reference
• Overpayments automatically flagged and credited to next term
Outstanding Balance Tracking
• Real-time fee balance dashboard: paid, pending, overdue, per student and per class
• Director/bursar sees school-wide collection rate at a glance on login
• Ageing analysis: balances overdue by 0–7 days, 7–30 days, 30+ days
• One-click export to PDF or Excel for board reporting
Automated Reminders
• Fee due date reminder: sent 7 days and 1 day before each payment deadline
• Overdue reminder: sent at 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days after due date
• Custom message templates configurable per school in English, Luganda, or other local languages
• Reminders sent via WhatsApp with SMS fallback; delivery status tracked

Two-Way Parent Interaction
Parents can send WhatsApp messages to the school's SchoolBridge number to self-serve basic requests:  BALANCE — returns current fee balance for their child  RECEIPT — sends the last payment receipt as a PDF  REPORT — sends the most recent term report card  HELP — returns a menu of available commands  This two-way functionality is a key differentiator from broadcast-only systems and dramatically reduces bursar workload.
5.3 Mobile Money Integration
SchoolBridge integrates with both MTN Mobile Money Uganda and Airtel Money Uganda via their respective APIs to enable parents to pay school fees from their mobile wallets without visiting the school.
Payment Flow
1. Parent receives WhatsApp reminder with unique student payment reference code
2. Parent dials *165# (MTN) or *185# (Airtel), selects Pay Bill, enters SchoolBridge merchant code and student payment reference
3. Mobile money operator processes payment and sends webhook notification to SchoolBridge API
4. SchoolBridge automatically credits the student fee account, generates a receipt, and sends WhatsApp confirmation to parent
5. Payment appears on bursar dashboard in real time — no manual entry required

5.5 Academic Module
The academic module digitises marks collection, grading, and report card generation with Uganda-specific standards built in.
Marks Entry
• Teachers access their class and subject marks entry form from any device
• Configurable assessment components: mid-term test, end-of-term exam, continuous assessment, practicals
• Marks saved immediately; no submit required — reduces data loss on connectivity drops
• Deputy head teacher or director can view marks entry progress per teacher in real time
Automated Grade Computation
• Totals, weighted averages, and final grades computed automatically on marks entry
• Grading scales configurable: Uganda national scale (D1–F9), percentage, or custom letter grades
• Class ranking and stream ranking computed per subject and overall
• Division computation for S4 and S6 following UNEB aggregation rules
UNEB-Format Report Cards
Uganda-Specific Advantage
SchoolBridge generates report cards in the exact formats required by the Uganda National Examinations Board for P7, UCE (S4), and UACE (S6) candidates. No foreign school management software offers this. It is the single most requested feature from Ugandan secondary school administrators.
• Primary leaving (P7) report card with all required UNEB fields
• UCE (O-Level) internal mock report in UNEB aggregate format
• UACE (A-Level) internal assessment report with principal and subsidiary pass/fail
• Internal primary/secondary report cards in configurable school-branded format
• Bulk PDF generation — all report cards for a class or entire school in one click
• Digital delivery via WhatsApp to parent + print-ready PDF for school records

7. Security & Compliance
SchoolBridge handles sensitive financial data, student academic records, and parent personal information. Security is a non-negotiable foundational requirement, not an afterthought.
7.1 Application Security
• JWT authentication with short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and secure refresh tokens (7 days)
• Role-based access control (RBAC) enforced at API middleware level — not just the UI
• Input validation and sanitisation on all user-submitted data using DRF serializers
• CSRF protection on all state-changing requests
• SQL injection prevention through Django ORM parameterised queries (no raw SQL)
• XSS prevention through React's DOM escaping and Content Security Policy headers
7.2 Data Security
• All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) — HTTPS enforced with HSTS
• Database encrypted at rest using cloud provider volume encryption (AES-256)
• Passwords hashed using Argon2
• Mobile Money API credentials stored in environment variables, never in source code
• Automated daily database backups with 30-day retention and point-in-time recovery
• Audit log on all administrative actions: who did what, when, from which IP
7.3 Regulatory Compliance
Compliance Requirements
SchoolBridge operates in compliance with:  Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (PDPA)  Bank of Uganda guidelines on mobile money payment services  Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) regulations for bulk messaging  Meta WhatsApp Business Terms of Service and Commerce Policy
• Parent data used only for school communication — never sold or shared with third parties
• Data retention policy: student records retained 7 years post-graduation; deleted on school request after exit

Pricing Philosophy
For schools hesitant to commit to a monthly subscription, SchoolBridge offers a fee-success pricing option: no monthly subscription, only 1.5% of fees collected via the platform. This removes the biggest objection and aligns SchoolBridge revenue directly with school collection success. Schools switch to subscription pricing once value is proven.

9.2 Pilot Success Criteria
The pilot with 3–5 schools will be declared successful when all three of the following metrics are achieved:
• Fee collection rate: equal to or greater than 85% of expected fees for the term
• Parent WhatsApp engagement: equal to or greater than 60% of parents have received and read at least one message
• Administrator time saved: equal to or greater than 5 hours per week reported by bursar or administrator
These three metrics become the core of the SchoolBridge sales pitch to all subsequent schools.
9.3 Pilot Rollout Steps
6. Select 3–5 schools with 200–800 students, mix of primary and secondary, Kampala and peri-urban
7. Two-day onboarding: data migration from existing registers, staff training, WhatsApp Business account setup
8. Week 1–2: manual payment recording only, staff familiarisation with the dashboard
9. Week 3: activate WhatsApp reminders, first automated message broadcast
10. Week 4–6: activate Mobile Money integration, first automated reconciliation
11. End of term: generate first digital report cards, measure all three success metrics
12. Debrief with school directors, collect testimonials, refine based on feedback
